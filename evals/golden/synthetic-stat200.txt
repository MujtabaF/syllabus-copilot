STAT 200 - Introduction to Statistical Inference (Fall 2026)

Welcome! This course covers sampling distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis
tests, and simple linear regression, with weekly computing work in R.

How you will be assessed

There are three in-class quizzes, each worth 5% of your grade. They will be held on
September 25, October 16 and November 13.

The term project is done in groups of three. Your group submits a two-page proposal
(5%) by September 30 and a final written report (20%) by December 4. Each group also
gives a short in-class presentation (5%) during the last week of classes; presentation
slots will be assigned later.

The midterm exam (25%) is on Wednesday, October 28. The cumulative final exam is worth
25% and will be held during the exam period; the date will be announced by the
registrar.
